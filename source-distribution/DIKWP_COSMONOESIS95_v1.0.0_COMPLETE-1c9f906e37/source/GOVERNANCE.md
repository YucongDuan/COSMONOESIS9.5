# Governance

## Principles

- One DIKWP coordinate space for all concepts.
- No permanent outside-space untranslatable residual.
- No automatic inference of historical or doctrinal identity.
- Branch-specific corrections are preferred over silent overwrite.
- High-impact judgments require at least two non-isomorphic models.
- Sources, uncertainty, burdens and authorization remain explicit.
- Automatic external-action authority remains zero.

## Registry changes

A proposal to change a tradition or civilization mapping should include:

1. affected term and branch;
2. primary or authoritative source context;
3. current vector and proposed vector;
4. explanation of D/I/K/W/P changes;
5. expected downstream effects;
6. dissenting or alternative mapping if material;
7. version and retirement conditions.

New concepts are never rejected as “outside DIKWP.” They are added under a versioned D, I, K, W or P parent with provisional confidence.

## Representation

No maintainer, contributor or output automatically represents a religion, culture, civilization, person, institution or humanity. Representation requires specific, named and revocable authorization.
